# Design review C (review4, round 4)

What was checked: `main` at 4e23d58 (the build the user installed), on branch `agent4/review4`.

- `CLEANCRAFT_SNAPSHOTS=1 cargo test -p cleancraft-ui-egui --test ux_render pages_render`: 35 scenes at 1060 x 680, 1280 x 820 and 1600 x 1000, 1x and 2x (identical to the committed `docs/screens/v2` renders), all looked at by eye and with sampled pixels.
- A kittest probe harness (not committed) that hovers every control on 19 screens and reads `platform_output.cursor_icon`, tabs through pages, focuses controls and diffs the rendered pixels, presses keys in the confirmation dialog, and saves frames 0 to 11 after Scan, after Review and Clean and after a sidebar click.
- `crates/ui-egui/tests/review_design_c.rs` (this branch): 13 `#[ignore]` tests, each failing on 4e23d58 with the measured value in its message. Run with `cargo test -p cleancraft-ui-egui --test review_design_c -- --ignored`.

Evidence crops are in `docs/design/review-c/`. Coordinates are points at 1x unless marked @2x. Contrast is the WCAG ratio of sampled render pixels.

The user's words: "the cursor does not change over buttons; the UX is better but still needs polish". Both are confirmed. The app now looks finished in still frames: one type scale, lit 3D objects, a calm sidebar. What separates it from the best Mac utilities is how it behaves under the pointer and the keyboard: nothing responds to the pointer's shape, focus is invisible, rows are not click targets, and a few surfaces still show egui's defaults (sliders, value boxes, the sort pop-up). After that come consistency between sections (five header styles, three bottom bars, three empty states) and the last truncations at 1060 pt.

Severity: S1 broken, S2 clearly unpolished, S3 nice to have. Owner: design4 (kit, theme, app, plan_page, sweep, extras pages, heroes) or ux4 (apps, startup, tasks, disk_map, monitor, history, settings pages). usability4 covers task flows in REVIEW-U; where a defect touches one of its U items it is cross-referenced, not repeated.

## Comparison with top Mac utilities (polish only)

Measured against the polish bar of the best paid Mac utilities, without borrowing their look:

- Pointer and focus (C1, C2, C21): those apps answer every pointer move and every Tab with a visible change; here hover is drawn but the pointer and focus say nothing. This is the gap the user felt first.
- Native-feeling details (C5, C11, C32, C35): a modal that owns the pointer, sliders and menus that look like the rest of the app, overlay scroll bars that hide at rest. Each egui default left on screen breaks the illusion of one designed product.
- Consistency (C15, C19, C20, C30): one header, one action bar and one empty state across the app is what makes a utility feel like one product rather than fifteen screens.
- Typography and fit (C4, C12, C13, C16, C17): names never cut, lists that say how much is hidden, no widows.

What is already at that level: the type scale, the colour fields per section, the 3D objects and their idle, scanning and done states, the dialog fade and rise, count-up and row stagger (once C10 is fixed), Reduce Motion support, contrast of text roles.
